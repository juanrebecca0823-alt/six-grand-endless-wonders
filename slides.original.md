\### Cultural \& Historical Insights

\## Heritage Context Behind Our Destinations



1\. \*\*Bangka Longshan Temple (1738):\*\* Built by Fujian settlers, blending Buddhist, Taoist, and folk deities; famous for intricate woodcarvings and \*Jiaobei\* moon-block divination.

2\. \*\*Shifen \& Jiufen Mining Towns:\*\* Shifen sky lanterns served as early safety signals; Jiufen prospered during the Japanese gold rush with iconic steep stairways and teahouses.

3\. \*\*TCM Acupuncture \& Ningxia Night Market:\*\* Traditional medicine balances \*Qi\* via meridian points; Ningxia preserves classic Taiwanese \*Banquet Snack\* heritage.

4\. \*\*Four Four South Village:\*\* Taipei’s first military dependents' village (\*Juancun\*), contrasting postwar low-rise homes directly beneath Taipei 101.

5\. \*\*Miyahara \& Shenji New Village:\*\* Adaptive reuse of a 1927 Japanese eye clinic and provincial government dorms into thriving confectionery and youth design hubs.

6\. \*\*Lukang Historic Port Town:\*\* Once Taiwan's second-largest port ("One Tainan, Two Lukang, Three Mengjia"), preserving red-brick alleys and wind-shielding \*Nine-Turns Lanes\*.
